Selected work

Score Desk gathers the score files for you. If the library is unavailable, it asks you to select the original PDF.

Proofread

Compare the current project score with the printed source and mark or describe errors. You can also record which pages you checked, including when you found no errors.

Send corrections to AI

Select Get correction package. Download the reports and instructions, attach the ZIP to your AI, and paste the instructions. Use Return AI response to send back the result.

Check AI corrections

Open an AI’s proposed version and compare its changes with the printed source. For each change you check, choose Looks correct, Still needs correction, or Needs clarification.

What the status means

  • Saved locally: a draft on this device.
  • Submitted to the project: your contribution is saved and has a receipt.
  • Awaiting review: the AI’s corrections are ready to check.
  • Applied to the project: the reviewed corrections are in the project score.
