About OPUS

Open Public-domain Universal Scores.

We turn printed public-domain scores into editable LilyPond, following the named edition.

Three agents, then a human

Two agents transcribe the print independently. A third reconciles their attempts against it. A human then compares the complete engraving with the original, bar by bar and staff by staff.

Public review drafts are labelled. A score becomes verified only after a human approves every staff and mark at an exact score hash. Compiling does not verify the music.

Source and records

Each work includes LilyPond, a rendered PDF, and records of its printed source, models and resolved readings. Original project files are CC0-1.0. Linked scans retain their own rights.
