# Source check: 2nd Recreation (Waltz)

Scouted 7 October 2026. Result: **no prior editable or modern transcription of this exact miniature was found in the checks below**. This is a bounded search result, not proof that nobody has transcribed it privately or elsewhere.

The sole musical source is the printed **2nd. RECREATION. / WALTZ.** on printed page 23, PDF page 25, of *Excelsior Method for the Reed Organ*, edited and compiled by T. H. Rollinson. The complete miniature occupies the bottom two grand-staff systems, 16 measures in G major, 3/4, ending at a final double bar. Exercises above it and the rest of the method are outside this work's scope. No composer is credited beside this miniature; Rollinson is credited as compiler of the volume, so the project does not assert individual authorship.
